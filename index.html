The game

Ialing: The New Frontier

Humanity was driven off Earth and scattered across the stars in escape pods. One of them came down on Ialing — a habitable world already spoken for, ringed by twenty-five moons and watched over by the largest of them.

You land with forty survivors and a damaged ship AI. Everything after that is yours to rebuild: shelter, food, research, defense. Other pods came down elsewhere. Finding them, and deciding who to stand with, is how a colony becomes something larger.

The studio

One person, one world

Gravity Line Studio LLC is an independent game studio registered in Wyoming. Everything here — design, code, art direction, and the writing — is made by a single developer, built and tested by hand on real hardware before anything ships.

The studio makes long games: worlds meant to be lived in over months rather than finished in an afternoon.
