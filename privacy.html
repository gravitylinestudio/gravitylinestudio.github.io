Privacy Policy

Effective28 July 2026
Version1.0
Applies toIaling: TNF

Short version: the game stores your progress so you don't lose it, sends you notifications if you allow them, and records anonymous usage and crash data so it can be fixed and improved. There are no ads, no advertising SDKs, and nothing is sold to anyone. You can delete your account and its data from inside the game at any time.

01Who this policy covers

This Privacy Policy explains how Gravity Line Studio LLC ("Gravity Line Studio", "we", "us") handles personal information in connection with the mobile game Ialing: The New Frontier (the "Game") and this website.

Gravity Line Studio LLC is a limited liability company registered in the State of Wyoming, United States. For any question about this policy or your information, contact gravitylinestudio@gmail.com.

02Information we collect

Account identifiers

The Game creates an account for you when you first play. By default this is an anonymous account: you are identified by a randomly generated Player ID issued by Unity Gaming Services. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number to play.

If you choose to link your account so it survives a device change, you supply a username and password. The password is handled and stored by Unity Gaming Services and is never visible to us.

Game progress

Your colony state — resources, buildings, research, population, alliance membership, chapter progress, settings, and similar gameplay data — is saved to Unity Cloud Save, tied to your Player ID.

Content you create

Your chosen display name, alliance name and description, alliance applications, and any messages you post in alliance chat or in-game forums are stored so other players can see them. Treat anything you type into a shared channel as public.

Device and usage information (automatically collected)

Through Google Firebase, the Game records:

  • Usage events — such as app opens, session length, screens viewed, and in-game milestones reached
  • Device and app information — device model, operating system version, language, country/region, app version, and build identifier
  • Approximate location — derived at a country or region level from your IP address. The Game does not request or use GPS or precise device location
  • Pseudonymous identifiers — a Firebase app instance ID and, where applicable, an advertising identifier used by Google Play services. These are not linked to your real-world identity by us

Crash and diagnostic data

Through Firebase Crashlytics, when the Game crashes or hits a serious error we receive a crash report containing the stack trace, device model and OS version, app version, memory and storage state at the time, a Crashlytics installation identifier, and a short log of recent in-game actions leading up to the failure.

Push notification token

If you permit notifications, Firebase Cloud Messaging issues a device token that lets us send you messages such as build completions, alliance activity, and event reminders. You can revoke this at any time in your device's notification settings.

What we do not collect

  • No precise or GPS location
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No payment information — the Game is free and contains no in-app purchases
  • No advertising SDKs and no ad targeting profiles

03How we use this information

  • To create and maintain your account and restore your progress across sessions and devices
  • To operate multiplayer features — alliances, applications, chat, forums, and leaderboards
  • To deliver notifications you have opted into
  • To diagnose crashes, fix bugs, and improve stability
  • To understand in aggregate how the Game is played, so that difficulty, pacing, and features can be tuned
  • To detect and prevent cheating, abuse, and violations of the Terms of Service
  • To comply with legal obligations

We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects about you.

04Service providers

We are a one-person studio and rely on established platforms to run the Game. These providers process data on our behalf under their own privacy commitments:

Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies)

Authentication and Cloud Save. Processes your Player ID, linked-account credentials, and saved game state.

Google Firebase (Google LLC)

Analytics, Crashlytics, and Cloud Messaging. Processes usage events, device information, IP-derived approximate location, crash reports, and notification tokens.

Google Play (Google LLC)

Distribution and app integrity. Google Play's own handling of your data is governed by Google's privacy policy, not this one.

We do not sell or share your personal information for money or for cross-context behavioural advertising, as those terms are defined under California law. We disclose information only to the providers above, or where required by law, or to protect the rights and safety of players and the studio.

05Legal bases (EEA and UK players)

  • Performance of a contract — creating your account, saving progress, and running multiplayer features, which are the service you asked for
  • Consent — analytics, crash reporting, and push notifications. You give this by accepting the notice shown before the Game signs you in for the first time, and by granting notification permission. You may withdraw consent as described in Section 7
  • Legitimate interests — keeping the Game secure, preventing abuse, and defending legal claims

06Retention

Account and save data is kept for as long as your account exists. If you delete your account, the associated authentication record and cloud save data are deleted from Unity Gaming Services.

Crash reports are retained by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days. Analytics event data is retained according to the Firebase Analytics retention setting, and aggregated statistics that cannot identify you may be kept indefinitely.

Content you posted publicly — such as forum or alliance messages — may remain visible after account deletion, since removing it would alter conversations other players took part in. Tell us if you need a specific post removed.

07Your choices and rights

Deleting your account

The Game includes account deletion in its settings. Deletion requires typing a confirmation and is permanent and unrecoverable — an anonymous account cannot be restored once removed. You can also request deletion by emailing gravitylinestudio@gmail.com.

Notifications

Turn off notifications for the Game in your device settings at any time. This stops delivery and invalidates the messaging token.

Analytics and crash reporting

To stop all analytics and crash collection, uninstall the Game. Android also offers device-level controls for resetting or limiting the advertising identifier.

Rights under GDPR and UK GDPR

If you are in the EEA or the UK you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to processing of your personal data, the right to data portability, and the right to withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing already carried out. You may also lodge a complaint with your national supervisory authority.

Rights under California law (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents may request to know what personal information we have collected, request its deletion or correction, and are entitled not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights. As stated above, we do not sell or share personal information, so no opt-out of sale is required.

Making a request

Email gravitylinestudio@gmail.com. We will respond within the period required by applicable law, generally 30 days. Because most accounts are anonymous, we may need your in-game Player ID to locate your data, and we may be unable to act on a request we cannot tie to an account.

08Children's privacy

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Players in the EEA must be at least 16, or the minimum age set by their country, to consent to the processing described here.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact gravitylinestudio@gmail.com and we will delete the account and its data.

09International transfers

Gravity Line Studio LLC operates from the United States, and our service providers process data on servers in the United States and other countries. If you play from outside the United States, your information will be transferred there. Where required, our providers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an equivalent transfer mechanism.

10Security

Data in transit is protected with industry-standard encryption, and account credentials are stored and verified by Unity Gaming Services rather than by us. No system is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law.

11Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the Game changes. The effective date and version at the top will change with it. If a change materially affects how we handle your information, the Game will ask you to review and accept the updated policy before you continue playing.
